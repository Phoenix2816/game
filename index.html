How to play

Guide your Welsh dragon to the glowing golden tankard. Each one adds one point and one body segment. A new tankard appears in an empty tile.

Stay inside the stone arena and avoid your own body. You can turn at right angles, but cannot reverse directly into your neck. Each body segment follows one tile. The game uses one pace: one move every 90 milliseconds, with a larger 20 by 20 field to explore.

Steer
Arrow keys or W A S D
Touch
Swipe on the arena or use the direction pad
Pause
Space, P, or the Pause button
Restart
R, New run, or Play again
Demo
Press 0 or Demo. The original demo logic chooses each turn by reachable food, free space, and distance. It restarts immediately after a loss. Press 0 again or Take control to steer the same dragon.

Phoenix stays at #1 with 189. Your scores appear below, ranked highest first. Grove, Dusk and Frost share this leaderboard. Scores are stored in this browser. Demo runs and runs taken over from a demo are practice; start a new run to save a score.

Opening a menu pauses your run; closing it resumes the run if you were playing. Switching tabs pauses the game until you choose to resume. The Space shortcut also works after using scenery and sound controls.
