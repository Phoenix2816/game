    A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

    How to play

    Guide your Welsh dragon to the glowing golden tankard. Each one adds one point and one body segment. A new tankard appears in an empty tile.

    Stay inside the stone arena and avoid your own body. You can turn at right angles, but cannot reverse directly into your neck. Your wings are decorative; collisions follow the tiled path of your body.

    Steer
    Arrow keys or W A S D
    Touch
    Swipe on the arena or use the direction pad
    Pause
    Space, P, or the Pause button
    Restart
    New run or the result screen

    Save your name after a run to join the local hall of fame. Scores are stored only in this browser. Demo runs do not count.

    The game pauses when you switch tabs or open a menu. Resume when you are ready.
